# Bright Smile Dental (demo clinic - fictional)

## Payment and insurance
We accept card payments (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept cheques.
Payment is taken at the end of each appointment.
We accept dental insurance from Bupa, AXA Health, Simplyhealth and Denplan. We provide receipts so you can claim from your insurer. Please check your cover with your insurer before treatment.
Interest-free finance (0%) is available for treatments over £1,000, over 6 or 12 months, subject to approval.

## Bright Smile Membership Plan
£19.50 per month for adults, £9.50 per month for children.
Includes 2 check-ups and 2 hygienist visits per year, 10% off other treatments, and worldwide dental emergency cover.

## New patients
We are welcoming new patients. New patients start with a new patient examination (£75), which includes 2 small X-rays and a treatment plan.
New patient offer: free consultation for Invisalign and teeth whitening.
Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit to fill in a medical history form. Bring a list of any medicines you take.

## Booking an appointment
You can request an appointment through this website chat, by phone on 020 7946 0321 or by email. Our team confirms the exact time by phone or text message.
We usually have check-up appointments within 1 week.

## Emergency dental care
We keep emergency appointments free every day for our patients and new patients. Please call us as early as possible on 020 7946 0321.
If you have severe swelling of the face or neck, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or bleeding that will not stop, call 999 or go to A&E.
Outside opening hours you can call NHS 111 for advice.

## Cancellation policy
Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move an appointment. Missed appointments or cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged a fee of £30.

## Children
We see children from their first tooth. Children's check-ups cost £35. Children under 16 must come with a parent or guardian.

## Nervous patients
Many of our patients feel nervous. Our team takes extra time to explain each step, and you can ask to stop at any time. Please tell us when you book if you feel anxious, so we can give you a longer appointment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does teeth whitening hurt?
Some people feel temporary tooth sensitivity during or after whitening. This usually goes away within a few days. Your dentist checks your teeth first to see if whitening is suitable for you.

### How long does Invisalign take?
Treatment time is different for each person. Many treatments take between 6 and 18 months. The free Invisalign consultation gives you a personal estimate.

### How long does a check-up take?
A routine check-up takes about 20 minutes. A new patient examination takes about 40 minutes.

### Do you offer sedation?
We do not offer sedation at this practice. For nervous patients we offer longer appointments and a calm, step-by-step approach.

### Do you offer NHS treatment?
No. Bright Smile Dental is a private practice only.

### Can I get an appointment on Saturday?
Yes, we are open every Saturday from 9:00am to 1:00pm. Saturday appointments are popular, so please request early.

### What languages do you speak?
Our team speaks English, Spanish, Polish and Urdu.

### Is there Wi-Fi in the waiting room?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available in the waiting room.

### Can I change my dentist?
Yes, you can ask for a specific dentist when you request an appointment.
